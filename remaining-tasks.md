# Remaining tasks for the finished exam assistant

This checklist covers **one full web-app build**, not a demo or reduced release. Every item below remains open until implemented and verified. [progress.md](progress.md) records what code already exists.

## Decisions that govern the work

- The bundled OS and IoT trees are active. Interpretation notes remain visible; there is no faculty approval gate.
- Uploads are stored when submitted. Explicit deletions execute immediately and are audited; neither waits for confirmation.
- Uncertain extraction or topic mapping must be shown and directly editable. It must never silently invent a unit, question or verdict.
- College paper patterns and custom templates arrive later. Their configuration, import and rendering flows still belong in the finished product.
- Chat is the primary interface; the classification engine must do the assessment work.
- Feedback propagation, precedent learning and model training remain future scope. Case-specific corrections may be recorded.
