# Comparison results

The comparison surface borrows the clarity of a marked question paper. Each result keeps the question, decision and reason together; the past-paper block and checks expand in place. The existing React workspace and its light and dark tokens provide the visual system. Paper-like surfaces, restrained serif question numbers and clear spacing keep long results readable. Disclosure arrows provide the only motion, with reduced-motion support. Results remain still when idle to preserve concentration.

## Typography

Instrument Sans carries navigation, controls, and reading text; Newsreader gives page headings and question numbers a restrained editorial voice. Body text starts at 16px, chat at 17px on desktop and 16px on phones, controls at 14px, and metadata at 12–13px. Page titles scale from 32–46px. Long answers stay within roughly 70 characters per line. Both families are self-hosted so the local workspace remains usable without a font service.

## Generated papers in chat

The generated draft fills the chat reading column. A compact title, stage, count and progress line lead; questions follow in syllabus-unit groups. Wide panes show two reading columns, while narrow panes show one. Question numbers use Newsreader and each question sits on a ruled row, preserving the feeling of a paper without nested cards. Screening notes stay beside their questions, and the composer remains aligned with the preview below the scrolling conversation.
